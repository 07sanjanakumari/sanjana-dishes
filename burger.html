Veg Burger

Ingredients

  • 2 Burger Buns
  • 1 Veg Patty
  • 2 Cheese Slices
  • 1 Tomato (sliced)
  • 1 Onion (sliced)
  • Lettuce Leaves
  • Tomato Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise
  • Butter

Recipe

  1. Cut the burger buns into two halves.
  2. Spread butter on the buns and toast them lightly.
  3. Cook the veg patty until it becomes golden brown.
  4. Spread mayonnaise and tomato ketchup on the buns.
  5. Place the lettuce, tomato slices, and onion slices on the bottom bun.
  6. Add the cooked veg patty.
  7. Place a cheese slice on top of the patty.
  8. Cover it with the top bun.
  9. Serve hot with French fries and tomato ketchup.
