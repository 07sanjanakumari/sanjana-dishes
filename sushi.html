Sushi

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sushi rice
  • 4 sheets of nori (seaweed)
  • 1 cucumber (cut into thin strips)
  • 1 carrot (cut into thin strips)
  • 1 avocado (sliced)
  • Fresh salmon or cooked shrimp (optional)
  • Soy sauce
  • Pickled ginger
  • Wasabi (optional)

Recipe

  1. Cook the sushi rice and let it cool.
  2. Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo sushi mat.
  3. Spread a thin layer of rice over the nori.
  4. Add cucumber, carrot, avocado, and salmon or shrimp in the center.
  5. Roll the sushi tightly using the bamboo mat.
  6. Cut the roll into small pieces with a sharp knife.
  7. Serve with soy sauce, pickled ginger, and wasabi.
