Masala Dosa

Ingredients

  • 2 cups dosa batter
  • 2 potatoes (boiled)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 green chilies (chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Few curry leaves
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Butter or ghee (optional)

Recipe

  1. Heat a non-stick pan or dosa tawa.
  2. Pour one ladle of dosa batter and spread it in a circular shape.
  3. Drizzle a little oil around the edges.
  4. Cook until the dosa becomes golden and crispy.
  5. In another pan, prepare the potato filling by cooking onions, green chilies, mustard seeds, curry leaves, turmeric, and mashed potatoes.
  6. Place the potato filling in the center of the dosa.
  7. Fold the dosa in half.
  8. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
