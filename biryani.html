Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500 g chicken
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1 cup curd (yogurt)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee

Recipe

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes, green chilies, turmeric, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
  5. Add the chicken and cook for 10–15 minutes.
  6. Mix in the curd, coriander, and mint leaves.
  7. Cook the soaked rice separately until it is about 70% cooked.
  8. Layer the rice over the chicken mixture.
  9. Cover the pan and cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes.
  10. Serve hot with raita or salad.
